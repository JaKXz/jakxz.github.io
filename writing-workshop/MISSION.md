# Mission: Argumentative technical essay craft

## Why

Finish “I attended the church of jj” in Jason’s own voice while building a
repeatable method for turning experience from technical work into clear,
arguable essays.

## Success looks like

- State one contestable thesis early enough that a reader knows what the essay
  will prove.
- Turn a real experience into a causal argument rather than a diary entry or a
  command reference.
- Revise structure and scope without flattening Jason’s humor, cadence, or
  judgment.
- Use automated prose feedback selectively: investigate each alert, but keep or
  reject it according to the essay’s argument and Jason’s voice.
- Publish the jj essay and reuse the same method on a later technical draft.

## Constraints

- Work through one short lesson and one section of the real draft at a time.
- Jason writes the prose; Codex teaches, questions, and gives focused feedback.
- Assume a Git-comfortable, jj-curious reader.
- Keep this essay story-led rather than turning it into a full jj tutorial.
- Keep the existing “Closing thoughts” unchanged unless Jason explicitly
  reopens it later.
- Run Vale after structural and read-aloud revision, never as a substitute for
  either one.
- Teaching materials live in this tracked `writing-workshop/` directory and do
  not become part of the website build.

## Out of scope

- Imitating Sean Goedecke’s sentence-level voice.
- Building a comprehensive jj alias or command reference inside the post.
- Blog growth, analytics, SEO, or publishing strategy.
- Rewriting the article on Jason’s behalf.
